Privacy law, computed and checkable.

A local engine for US federal and New York privacy law. It answers as of a date, computes what recall cannot, and produces artifacts you can verify, diff and re-run.

Every provision is quoted verbatim from primary source and carries the SHA‑256 of the bytes it was verified against. Nothing is synthesised at query time, and nothing leaves your machine.

01 · Answer

As of a date, or not at all.

There is no “current law” here. Every query resolves against a date. Exemptions are typed objects rather than flags, because entity‑level and data‑level relief are not the same thing. Preemption is resolved, not assumed.

A clock starts only when its own trigger is dated. An obligation whose trigger has no date is reported as not started — never given a borrowed one, which is how four unrelated clocks come to share a single wrong date.

privacy-kb ask --hipaa --ny-data --breach --told-hhs
Obligations in force as of 2026-09-16 — 18 engaged

  45 C.F.R. § 164.404(a)   notify each affected individual
  45 C.F.R. § 164.408(a)   notify the Secretary of HHS
  GBL § 899-aa(2)          notify New York residents
  GBL § 899-aa(9)          notify the NY Attorney General

  exemption  § 899-aa(2)(b) is activity_level, not entity_level:
             it removes notice to people already notified
             federally and preserves notice to the AG
  preemption 45 C.F.R. § 160.203 — HIPAA is a FLOOR, so the
             more stringent state duty survives

Real output. Exemption type and preemption posture are fields, not prose.

02 · Compute

What recall cannot do.

Four operations that are arithmetic over a model, not recollection of a document.

exposure sweeps roughly 500 applicability evaluations across 126 fact keys: which facts would change this answer. Enumerated, not sampled.

between diffs two dates from stored vintages, and separates three things a single “changed” would collapse: what commenced, what the corpus cannot attribute to a dated legal event, and what it could not be asked about at all.

requirements enumerates every element a provision imposes against the segmentation of its own source file, so the denominator is declared rather than implied. conform lays those elements beside your document — with the verdict column deliberately empty.

privacy-kb between 2022-06-01 2023-06-01 --nyc-hiring
Commenced — 3
  + N.Y.C. Admin. Code § 20-871(a)  (effective 2023-01-01, stated_in_text)
  + N.Y.C. Admin. Code § 20-871(b)  (effective 2023-01-01, stated_in_text)
  + N.Y.C. Admin. Code § 20-872(b)  (effective 2023-01-01, stated_in_text)

Differs, cause unattributable — 6
  ? 6 RCNY § 5-301(a)  appeared; effective_from is a capture
    date (undetermined), not a commencement

39 obligation(s) could not be asked about this window: the only
text held for them is younger than 2022-06-01.

A difference between two dates is not automatically a change in the law.

03 · Prove

Artifacts, not prose.

Prose expires the moment anything moves. These outputs persist, re-run identically, and can be handed to a third party.

receipt issues a digest anyone can verify — including you, later, without trusting you. memo produces a citable record of a whole analysis, every provision with its URL, hash and vintage. Same inputs, byte‑identical output, across separate processes.

profile keeps a standing fact set and a register. The question in‑house counsel actually has is “this says something different than last quarter — did the law change, or did we?”, and it is answerable because the four causes are recorded separately.

privacy-kb profile show acme
acme · Acme Health, NY
  state layers: US-NY
  entity: is_hipaa_covered_entity=true, nexus="US-NY"

Register  3 check(s)
  2026-09-16  as of 2022-06-01    2 obligation(s)  baseline
  2026-09-16  as of 2023-06-01   11 obligation(s)  date+result
  2026-09-16  as of 2026-09-16   11 obligation(s)  date+result

corpus · facts · date · result — recorded separately, so a
moved answer can be attributed rather than guessed at.

Editing the facts preserves the register. It is the evidence that explains the next answer.

04 · Audit

Check an answer this engine did not write.

Paste any assistant’s answer, a memo, or a letter from the other side. Every authority in it is checked: does the citation resolve, was it in force on that date, is it enacted‑but‑pending or superseded, and — with facts — does it reach the entity being advised.

That last check is the one a click‑through cannot perform. A fabricated citation fails the first check anybody runs. A real citation attached to a proposition it does not support survives every check anybody runs.

Two things it never says. It never reports a claim correct: whether the quoted words support the proposition is a reading, and this engine does not make readings. The clean status is NO_PROBLEM_FOUND, never “verified”. And it never reports a citation fabricated: coverage is federal, New York State and New York City, so a real provision outside that scope resolves to nothing for the same reason an invented one would.

privacy-kb ground --text answer.txt --as-of 2026-09-16
✗ N.Y. Gen. Bus. Law § 1501(1)    PENDING_NOT_BINDING
    enacted but not in force until 2027-01-25; citing it
    for what is required today asserts a duty that binds
    nobody yet

✗ 45 C.F.R. § 164.404(b)          DOES_NOT_REACH_THESE_FACTS
    predicate: entity.is_hipaa_covered_entity (false)
               == true -> false

? Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.82        CITATION_NOT_HELD
    not held here — a statement about this corpus, NOT a
    finding that the citation is fabricated

Citations are extracted from prose lexically. What a citation was offered for is left to the reader, who has the paragraph.

Where most of the work went

What it refuses to tell you.

A confident answer over a gap is worse than a smaller answer that names the gap. These refusals are enforced in code and exercised in CI, not left to convention.

It will not answer without a date

A malformed date is refused too, because date comparisons are string comparisons: a typo would otherwise sort above every real date and quietly report law that is not yet in force. as_of: "not-a-date" → refused

It will not treat enacted law as binding early

A pending record routes to a watch feed and cannot become an obligation at any as‑of date until a person promotes it. Nothing silently becomes law.

It will not hide what it cannot reach

Coverage gaps, element shortfall against a source’s own segmentation, and date windows it cannot be asked about are returned with the answer, not in a report beside it.

It will not guess a preemption posture

Where the statute does not speak to the question, the posture is reported as unresolved rather than inferred from the shape of the statute.

It will not quote what it could not verify

A record that fails verification against its stored source is suppressed from every output — by the loader, rather than by each caller remembering to filter.

It will not borrow a date for a clock

An obligation whose trigger has no date is reported as not started. One event date applied to every trigger produces four confident, unrelated deadlines.

What is actually in it

Coverage is declared before it is measured.

Instrument by instrument, so the corpus knows what it is missing. Every figure here is generated from the repository — a hand‑typed count drifts.

1,581provisions held
226analysed obligations
54instruments
50CI gates

Two different claims, and the difference matters. 1,581 provisions are held as verified text: quoted verbatim from the primary source, hash‑anchored, with a citation and a dated vintage. 226 of them are analysed — an applicability predicate has been written, and the engine reasons with those and only those. A provision with no predicate can never make the engine assert that a duty applies to anyone, which is enforced by the loader rather than by convention.

New York is built, not bolted on. SHIELD, the Child Data Protection Act and its OAG guidance, the SAFE for Kids Act, GBL §§ 349/350, Education Law § 2‑d, Civil Rights Law §§ 50/51 and § 52‑c, Penal Law §§ 250.00/250.05, and NYC Local Law 144 with its 6 RCNY implementing rules.

The corpus is plain files in git — one record per file, no database — and 50 CI gates run over it on every change. They check that a quoted span appears in the stored source bytes, that a paragraph path resolves to exactly one leaf and agrees with its own citation, that a version chain has no gaps, and that a date’s basis is derived from the source rather than declared. 37 are exercised by fixtures built to trip them; the other 13 are declared unexercisable, with the reason, so a new gate cannot quietly join the untested set.

Getting it running

Two ways to use it.

A command‑line tool, and an MCP server exposing 26 tools — so an assistant you already use can call the engine instead of recalling the law.

Install Node 20 or newer

From nodejs.org. Nothing else is required, and nothing calls out to a network at query time.

Get the project and install

git clone https://github.com/rakib-nyc/privacy-kb.git
cd privacy-kb
npm install

Check the install, then register it

The second command writes the MCP client configuration. Restart the client afterwards.

npm run doctor
npm run setup -- --write

Ask something you can check

Every answer carries its citations, hashes and dates, so the first thing to do with one is verify it.

privacy-kb ask --hipaa --ny-data --breach --told-hhs
privacy-kb cite us.hipaa.breach.404a.notify_individuals

Where it is weak.

Published because a tool that hides these is less trustworthy than one that does not.

Narrow by design

US federal, New York State, New York City. No other state. No GDPR beyond transfer‑mechanism touchpoints. Almost no case law.

Thin in places, and it says where

It holds 1 of the 129 elements beneath 15 U.S.C. § 1681g. Element shortfall is computed per duty category and travels with the answer — but a gap register describes a hole, it does not fill one.

529 records cannot anchor an as‑of comparison

Their date records when the text was captured, not when the law began. Differences across such a date are reported as unattributable rather than as change.

Business days mean weekdays

Public holidays are not excluded, so a clock crossing one lands a day early. Every affected result says so in the output.

Pending law does not promote itself

That is the point, and it also means the corpus needs maintaining: the SAFE for Kids Act commences 25 January 2027.

Coverage is 31 of 69 subject areas

38 are neither covered nor ruled out of scope. That number is tracked and may only go down. Nine instruments hold a single record each: complete against what they declare, but thin.
